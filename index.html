BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little clarity.

Still wondering about something?
Ask us directly ↗

What does DuoFaith actually lock?+

Only the apps you choose. Set a daily lock time and DuoFaith uses Apple’s Screen Time controls to shield those apps until you finish your devotional. Keep essential apps out of your selection so they stay available.

What’s in a daily devotional?+

A Bible passage, a short teaching, one reflection question, and a prayer. It takes a few minutes. On your own, reflection is a quiet pause. When paired, you write an answer to share with your partner.

How does Couples Mode work?+

Pair two iPhones using an invite code. You each answer the same daily reflection question. Your partner’s answer stays hidden until you submit yours; once both have answered, you can read each other’s responses. Days when you both answer build your shared streak. Couples Mode requires Pro.

Do I have to wait for my partner to unlock my apps?+

No. Your own devotional unlocks your own selected apps. You can finish at different times. The shared part is the reflection: both answers become available after you have both written.

Is DuoFaith free?+

DuoFaith is free to download. Continued app blocking after introductory access and Couples Mode require Pro. Current prices and any introductory access are shown in the app before you subscribe. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account.

Can I use it on my own?+

Yes. Daily Scripture, reflection, prayer, and your personal reading streak work as an individual routine. Pairing is optional. DuoFaith is available for iPhone; check the App Store for current device compatibility.
