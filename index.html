Questions, answered.

How does the app blocking work?

+

DuoFaith uses your device's built-in screen time controls to block apps you choose. Each morning, they stay locked behind a Bible verse and reflection prompt. Complete it, and your apps unlock for the day.

Do I need a partner to use DuoFaith?

+

No. DuoFaith works great solo. The couples feature is a Pro perk — pair up if you want mutual accountability, or use it on your own.

Is my data private?

+

Your data stays on your device. We don't sell your information. The only thing shared is your completion status with your paired partner, if you choose to pair.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

+

Yes. Cancel anytime through your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. You'll keep Pro features until the end of your billing period.

What happens if I don't finish my devotional?

+

Your selected apps stay locked until you complete it. That's the whole point — DuoFaith gives you the structure that willpower alone can't.
