How it works

How the Bible app blocker works

Set it up once. After that, every morning runs the same way: your apps lock, you read, they open.

1

Pick the apps that pull you in

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, games. You choose the list from Apple's own app picker. Anything you do not pick is never touched.

2

Set your lock time

The default is 8 AM. Every day at that time your chosen apps are shielded. Try to open one and you see a lock screen instead of a feed.

3

Read to unlock

Open DuoFaith and read today's devotional: a Bible passage, a short plain-language lesson, one reflection question and a prayer. About two minutes.

4

Finish, unlock, keep the streak

Tap Finish and everything opens. Your streak goes up by one. Miss a morning and it resets, so the habit has real weight.

Couples Mode

Couples Mode: neither iPhone unlocks until both have read

Pair two iPhones with an invite code. Both lock each morning, and neither unlocks until you have both finished, so reading first does not free your own apps. You can see whether your partner has read yet and send one gentle nudge a day. It works for married, engaged or dating couples, or any two accountability partners. Couples Mode needs Pro.

  • Live status: waiting, partner done, both done
  • One gentle nudge a day, never nagging
  • A shared streak. If one of you skips, it breaks for both
  • Married, engaged, dating, or any two accountability partners

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is there an app that locks your apps until you read the Bible?

Yes. DuoFaith locks the iPhone apps you choose every morning at a time you set, and keeps them shielded until you finish a short devotional of about two minutes. It uses Apple's own Screen Time controls, so there is no Ignore button. Finish the passage, one reflection question and a prayer, and your apps unlock for the day.

Learn how the lock works

What does Bible before phone mean?

Bible before phone means you open Scripture before you open anything else on your phone. No messages, no news, no feeds until you have read. Justin Whitmel Earley calls it Scripture before phone in The Common Rule. The idea is that whatever you take in first shapes your whole morning, so you let God speak before the world does.

Read the Bible before phone guide

How do I stop scrolling and read the Bible instead?

Do not try to quit scrolling. Replace it. Keep the cue, which is picking up your phone, and swap the routine for one short passage. Keep your apps as the reward afterwards. Make the reading tiny so you can do it on a bad day. If the swap keeps failing, DuoFaith locks the apps you pick until you have read, with no Ignore button.

Can an app really help with phone addiction as a Christian?

Yes, for one specific part of the problem. An app cannot change your heart, and it is not a recovery program. What it can do is remove the 7am argument. DuoFaith keeps the apps you choose locked each morning until you finish a short devotional. There is no Ignore button. You decide once, at night, and the morning follows.

Is there a screen time app for couples that locks both partners' apps?

Yes. DuoFaith has a Couples Mode that links two iPhones. Each of you picks the apps that distract you and a lock time.
