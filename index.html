Questions

How is this different from a devotional app?

+

Most devotional apps give you a verse and hope you read it. DuoFaith actually locks your distracting apps until you finish a full Bible passage. You can't skip it — and if you're paired with your partner, they can't either.

Can I use DuoFaith without a partner?

+

Yes. Solo mode works great — your apps lock until you complete the reading, same as couples. The partner features are available on Pro when you're ready to pair up.

What happens if my partner doesn't read?

+

Your apps stay locked too. That's the point — mutual accountability. You can send them a nudge notification to remind them.

Is my data private?

+

Your data stays on your device. We don't sell your information. The only thing shared is your reading status with your paired partner.

Does Pro cover both me and my partner?

+

Yes. One Pro subscription at $4.99/month covers both partners. No need to pay twice.
