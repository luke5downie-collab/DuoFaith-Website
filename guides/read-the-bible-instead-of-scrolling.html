Make your next scroll a moment in Scripture.

A practical way to replace the first social-media check with a daily Bible devotional in DuoFaith.

A practical way to replace the first social-media check with a daily Bible devotional in DuoFaith.

Keep the starting point familiar.

You already pick up your phone. Give yourself a different place to go next: one passage, one reflection, one prayer. A routine you can complete is easier to return to than an ambitious plan you keep putting off.

Choose the feeds that can wait.

You do not have to put your whole phone away to set aside time for Scripture. Decide which apps interrupt the moment you want to keep. DuoFaith lets you select those apps and set a daily lock time, while unselected apps remain available.

Finish a devotional, then carry on.

DuoFaith’s daily devotional takes a few minutes. Read the passage and short teaching, reflect on a question, and pray. Finishing unlocks your selected apps. Your personal reading streak helps you see the days you have shown up.

Make it a shared conversation.

Your selected apps unlock when you finish your own devotional. Couples Mode adds a shared reflection: each of you writes an answer to the same question, and your partner’s answer stays hidden until you submit yours. Once you have both answered, both reflections are available. Days when both of you answer build a shared streak.

Download details.

DuoFaith starts with a 3-day free trial. After that it is $10 a month or $50 a year, and one subscription covers everything, Couples Mode included. There is no free tier, so cancel inside the 3 days if you decide it is not for you.
