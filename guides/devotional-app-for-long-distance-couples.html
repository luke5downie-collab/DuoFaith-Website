LONG-DISTANCE COUPLES

Different places. The same daily question.

Stay connected through DuoFaith’s shared written reflections, even when your daily routines happen apart.

By Luke Downie · Updated September 15, 2026

Stay connected through DuoFaith’s shared written reflections, even when your daily routines happen apart.

Share a small part of your day.

When you are apart, a simple question can give a conversation somewhere to begin. DuoFaith is a daily devotional and app blocker each of you uses on your own; Couples Mode pairs your iPhones on top of that, so you can read Scripture separately and share a written reflection. You do not need to be in the same room or finish at the same moment.

Write first. Read each other next.

Your partner’s response is hidden until you submit your own. When both answers are in, you can read both. Use the reflection as a starting point for a call or message, or simply as a way to understand what the other person is carrying.

Your phone follows your own reading.

Your selected apps unlock when you finish your own devotional. Couples Mode adds a shared reflection: each of you writes an answer to the same question, and your partner’s answer stays hidden until you submit yours. Once you have both answered, both reflections are available. Days when both of you answer build a shared streak.

Get connected.

Couples Mode is included in your subscription. One person generates an invite code and sends it to the other. The other joins using the six-digit code before it expires, within 24 hours. Your reflections are shared with the person you pair with, so choose someone you intend to read them.

Before you start.

DuoFaith starts with a 3-day free trial. After that it is $10 a month or $50 a year, and one subscription covers everything, Couples Mode included. There is no free tier, so cancel inside the 3 days if you decide it is not for you.
