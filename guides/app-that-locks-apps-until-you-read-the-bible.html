An app that locks your apps until you read the Bible (and how it works on iPhone)

Quick answer

Yes. DuoFaith is an iPhone app that locks the apps you choose every morning and keeps them shielded until you finish a short devotional, about two minutes long. It uses Apple's own Screen Time controls. There is no Ignore button. The only way back to your apps is through four short screens of Scripture.

If you have searched for an app that locks your apps until you read the Bible, you probably already know the pattern. You pick up your phone to check one thing. Half an hour later you are still scrolling, and your Bible is still closed.

DuoFaith is built for exactly that moment. It is a Bible app that locks your phone's most distracting apps each morning, and it keeps them locked until you have read.

This guide explains how it works on iPhone, what Apple's built-in Screen Time can and cannot do, and how to set it up in a few minutes.

How an app that locks your apps until you read the Bible actually works

DuoFaith uses Apple Screen Time, the same system behind Downtime and App Limits, to shield the apps you pick. You choose the apps. You choose a lock time, and 8:00 AM is the default.

At that time each day, those apps go behind a shield. If you tap Instagram, you do not get your feed. You get a screen that says: Faith over distraction. Complete your devotional to unlock this app.

There is no Ignore button on that screen. There is no test either. DuoFaith does not quiz you. It works for a simpler reason: the only way to your apps is through four short screens of Scripture, and you have to reach the end.

When you tap Finish & Unlock, every shielded app opens again for the rest of the day. The unlock happens when you finish, not at a clock time. You can see the whole flow on the homepage.

Why not just use Screen Time Downtime?

Fair question. Apple's built-in Screen Time is free and already on your iPhone. You can set Downtime for a window, say 7:00 to 8:00 AM, and only the apps you allow will work in that window. You can also set App Limits on specific apps. Apple explains both on its Screen Time support page.

First, the Ignore Limit tap. When a limit kicks in, iOS offers you one more minute, 15 more minutes, or ignoring the limit for the day. That one tap is where most morning plans quietly end.

Second, Downtime is tied to the clock, not to you. It gives you one window per day, and it ends at a set time whether you opened your Bible or not. It cannot tell the difference.

So if all you want is a fixed quiet window, Downtime can do that for free. If you want the unlock tied to actually reading, that is the gap DuoFaith fills. The apps stay shielded until the devotional is done.

What you read before your apps unlock (about two minutes)

The devotional is short on purpose.

Each day you get a new one. It has a title and theme, a Bible passage with its reference, a little context, a plain-language lesson and a takeaway. Then comes one reflection question. The app asks you to sit with it for a moment, about 15 seconds, before you can move on. There is nothing to type and nothing is saved. Last comes a short written prayer, and then the Finish & Unlock button.

Scripture is in the World English Bible, the only translation in the app right now.

Your word is a lamp to my feet, and a light for my path. Psalm 119:105 (WEB)

Devotionals are cached, so a weak signal in the morning does not keep you locked out. The whole thing takes about two minutes. If you want help making that two minutes stick, read Bible before phone.

What this Bible app that locks apps does not do

  • It only locks the apps you pick. Calls, texts, maps, your bank, and anything you did not choose are never touched.
  • It locks once a day, at your lock time. It does not lock again every time you open an app. Finish once and you are done until tomorrow.
  • It is not a parental control. You set it up for yourself, on your own phone. It does not monitor anyone and it does not show usage reports.
  • It is not a cage. Deleting the app removes the lock. DuoFaith is a commitment you choose each day, not a wall you cannot get past.
  • It is iPhone only. You need iOS 26.2 or later. There is no Android version.

Pricing is simple. DuoFaith is free to download, and every new user starts with a free trial of Pro. Couples Mode needs Pro, at $9.99 a month or $49.99 a year.

Lock two phones at once with Couples Mode

DuoFaith works fully on its own. But if you and your spouse, fiancé or a friend both fight the same morning scroll, Couples Mode links two iPhones.

Both phones lock each morning. Neither unlocks until you have both finished the day's devotional. Finishing first does not free your own phone. You can see whether your partner has read yet, and send one gentle nudge a day. You share one streak, and if one of you skips, it breaks for both.

Each of you still picks your own apps and your own lock time. You read the same passage, on your own phones. You can unpair any time and go back to solo.

How to lock your apps until you read the Bible: step by step

  1. Download DuoFaith

    Get DuoFaith from the App Store. It is free to download. No account, no email, no password. Start the free trial of Pro when the app asks, and you are in.

  2. Pick the apps that steal your morning

    Choose the apps to shield using Apple's Screen Time picker. Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, whatever pulls at you. Leave calls, texts and maps alone. Only what you pick gets locked.

  3. Set your lock time

    The default is 8:00 AM. Change it to fit your morning. From that time each day, your chosen apps stay shielded until your devotional is done.

  4. Tomorrow morning, tap Begin Reading

    When you hit a shielded app, you will see the shield screen. Open DuoFaith instead and tap Begin Reading. Read the passage, the context and the lesson.

  5. Pause on the question, pray, then unlock

    There is nothing to type. Just sit with the one reflection question for a moment, then read the prayer. Tap Finish & Unlock and your apps open for the rest of the day. Your streak goes up by one.

  6. Optional: pair with your partner

    In Couples Mode, one of you creates an invite code and the other enters it before it expires. From then on, neither phone unlocks until you have both read.

Common questions

Does it lock everything on my phone, including calls and texts?

No. DuoFaith only shields the apps you choose in the Screen Time picker. Calls, texts, maps, your camera and every app you did not select keep working as normal. You can change the list any time in Settings, so if you find you left something out, add it the next day.

Can you lock apps until a certain time on iPhone instead?

Yes, with Apple's built-in Screen Time. Downtime blocks apps for one fixed window a day and ends at a set time, and App Limits cap daily use. DuoFaith works differently. Your apps unlock when you finish the devotional, not when the clock says so. If you want a fixed window, use Downtime. If you want the unlock tied to reading, use DuoFaith.

Is there an app that "forces you to read the Bible"?

No app can truly force you. DuoFaith does not test you or quiz you. It simply removes the easy way out: your chosen apps stay shielded, there is no Ignore button, and the only path back is four short screens of Scripture. You could always delete the app. The point is that you decide once, the night before.

Which iPhones and Bible translations does it work with?

DuoFaith runs on iPhone only and needs iOS 26.2 or later. There is no Android version. Scripture is in the World English Bible, a public domain translation. It is the only translation in the app right now, so check that before you download if you need a specific one.

Do I have to type an answer to the reflection question?

No. The reflection step is one question and a short pause of about 15 seconds. The app asks you to sit with it for a moment, then lets you continue. Nothing is typed and nothing is saved. It is meant to be a quiet beat in your morning, not homework.
