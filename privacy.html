Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 17, 2026

DuoFaith ("the App") is developed by Luke Downie ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. We built DuoFaith with privacy as a core principle — your data stays on your device.

Information We Collect

DuoFaith stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Your first name (entered during onboarding)
  • Your chosen lock time (hour and minute)
  • Devotional progress (current streak, total devotionals completed, date of last devotional)
  • Onboarding completion status
  • Pro/Free plan status

This data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework and never leaves your device.

Screen Time and Family Controls

DuoFaith uses Apple's Screen Time API (Family Controls / Managed Settings) to shield selected apps until you complete your daily devotional. The App requests your authorization to use these system frameworks. App-shielding selections are handled entirely by Apple's on-device APIs — we never see or transmit which apps you choose to shield.

Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications to remind you of your daily devotional. These notifications are scheduled on-device and are not sent through any external server.

Data We Do NOT Collect

DuoFaith does not:

  • Collect or transmit any personal data to external servers
  • Use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • Require account creation, sign-up, or authentication
  • Collect email addresses, location data, or device identifiers
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use cookies or web-based tracking

Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete all your progress data at any time using the "Reset Progress" option in Settings. To delete all data completely, uninstall the App from your device — this removes all stored information.

Children's Privacy

DuoFaith does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
